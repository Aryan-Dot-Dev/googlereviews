Before you commit

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. The card opens Google's own review form on the customer's phone. Customers read, edit or rewrite the review before posting it. Nothing is posted automatically and no rating is suggested.

What does the customer see when they tap?

A clean page with your business name, a suggested review they can edit, and one button that copies it and opens your Google review form. Nothing is installed and no app is needed.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. NFC tap to open works in the phone's browser on modern iPhones and Android phones. Every card also ships with a QR code printed on the back for phones without NFC.

Can a customer edit the review before posting?

Always. The suggested review is a starting point. The page tells the customer they can edit it before posting, and they can rewrite it completely if they want.

What if a phone does not support NFC?

Every card ships with a QR code printed on the back that opens the same review page, so no customer is turned away.

How much does it cost?

Simple flat pricing per location, with no monthly fee. Tell us about your business through the form and we will send you the exact price for your setup.

How long does setup take?

We usually deliver within a week. Nearby businesses get a store visit where we handle everything in person; everyone else gets their card by delivery.

What do you need from me?

Just your Google Business Profile listing. We find your review link, write the suggested review, print the card and set it up. You place it at the counter.

Where is the data stored?

Your enquiry is stored in our database so we can contact you. We use it only for this and never share it. See the privacy policy linked in the footer.
