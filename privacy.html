Privacy

Last updated September 2026

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our site, we store the details you enter: your name, phone number, business name, city, and anything you write in the message field. We also store your browser's user agent string for support diagnostics.

What we don't collect

We don't run advertising trackers on this site. Customer review pages opened by NFC taps don't collect anything at all — the review text is copied on the customer's own phone and posted to Google directly.

How your data is used

Only to contact you about your enquiry and provide the review card service. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

Reviews posted by customers

Reviews are posted by customers to their own Google accounts. We never post reviews on anyone's behalf and we don't have access to the content of reviews customers write or edit.

Data storage and deletion

Enquiries are stored in a managed cloud database. Ask us at any time and we'll delete your enquiry.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the enquiry form on the main page.
