Terms

The service

TapCard provides businesses with an NFC card and a hosted review page. Tapping the card opens the business's own Google review form with a suggested review that the customer can edit or rewrite before posting.

What we don't do

  • We never post reviews on anyone's behalf.
  • We never suggest ratings or write reviews that misrepresent an experience.
  • We never ask customers for a specific star rating.

The customer stays in full control of what they post. The card only makes Google's own review form easier to reach.

Customer responsibility

Businesses agree to use the card as a convenience for genuine customers and to follow Google's review policies. Encouraging reviews in exchange for payment, discounts or perks is not allowed.

Payment and delivery

Pricing is flat per location with no recurring fee. We confirm the exact price before any payment is due, and you see a live demo of your review page before you pay.

Data

Enquiry details are used only to contact you about your order. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Contact

Questions about these terms: reach us through the enquiry form on the main page.
